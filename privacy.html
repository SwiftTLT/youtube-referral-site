Privacy Policy

YT-Referral — last updated 2026-10-09

YT-Referral is a personal automation tool built and run by its one operator, for that operator's own use only. It is never distributed to or used by anyone else, and it does not create accounts, collect information from visitors to this page, or use cookies or analytics.

What Google data this app accesses

This app requests one Google OAuth scope: https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube ("Manage your YouTube account"), used only to:

  • List the videos in a small number of the operator's own YouTube playlists;
  • Look up public metadata (title, channel, description, duration) for those videos and for a short list of channels the operator chooses to monitor;
  • Add videos from those monitored channels to one of the operator's own playlists;
  • Remove a video from a playlist once it has been processed.

This app never posts, comments, likes, subscribes, uploads video, or changes any account setting. It never accesses any YouTube account other than the operator's own.

What this app does with that data

Video titles, descriptions, and publicly available captions are sent to an AI summarization service (Anthropic's Claude) solely to generate a written summary. The summary and a small amount of bookkeeping data (video ID, title, channel, status) are stored in a local database and a local notes file, both kept on the operator's own equipment and never uploaded anywhere else or shared with any third party. The operator may also receive a summary notification through a private Telegram bot that only the operator controls.

No data this app accesses is sold, rented, used for advertising, or used to train any model beyond the ordinary processing of a single summarization request.

Data retention and deletion

All stored data is local to the operator's own equipment and under the operator's sole control. The operator can delete any or all of it at any time by deleting the local database, notes, or cached files. Revoking this app's access from the operator's Google Account permissions page immediately stops any further access.

Contact

This app has a single operator and no public users. Any question about it can be directed to the support email address listed on its Google OAuth consent screen.
